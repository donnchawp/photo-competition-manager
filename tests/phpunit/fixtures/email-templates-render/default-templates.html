Email Templates

Customize the email templates sent to members. Use merge tags to personalize messages.

Upload link

Sent to members with a link to upload their images.

Available merge tags:

  • {member_name} The member's name.
  • {competition_title} The competition's title.
  • {site_name} This site's name.
  • {upload_link} The member's own upload link.
  • {voting_page} The voting page.

Voting opened

Sent to every member when voting opens in a category.

Available merge tags:

  • {member_name} The member's name.
  • {competition_title} The competition's title.
  • {site_name} This site's name.
  • {voting_page} The voting page.
  • {close_date} The competition's close date.

Voting link

Sent to a member who asks for a voting link on the voting page.

Available merge tags:

  • {member_name} The member's name.
  • {competition_title} The competition's title.
  • {site_name} This site's name.
  • {voting_link} The member's own voting link.
  • {close_date} The competition's close date.

Results published

Sent to members with a private link to the results.

Available merge tags:

  • {member_name} The member's name.
  • {competition_title} The competition's title.
  • {site_name} This site's name.
  • {results_page} The private link to the results.
  • {results_share_link} The same as {results_page}.

Detailed results

Sent to each member who entered, with the ranks, scores and votes for their images.

Available merge tags:

  • {member_name} The member's name.
  • {competition_title} The competition's title.
  • {site_name} This site's name.
  • {results_table} The ranks, scores and votes for the member's images.

Submission confirmed

Sent to a member each time they upload an image.

Available merge tags:

  • {member_name} The member's name.
  • {competition_title} The competition's title.
  • {site_name} This site's name.
  • {member_grade} The member's grade.
  • {category_name} The category the image was uploaded to.
  • {current_count} How many images the member has uploaded to the category.
  • {quota} How many images the category takes from each member.
