Email Templates

Customize the email templates sent to members. Use merge tags to personalize messages.

Upload Reminder

Sent to members with a link to upload their images.

Available merge tags: {member_name}, {competition_title}, {upload_link}, {voting_page}, {site_name}

Voting Opened

Sent when voting opens for a competition.

Available merge tags: {member_name}, {competition_title}, {voting_page}, {close_date}, {site_name}

Competition Closed

Sent when a competition closes (voting ends).

Available merge tags: {member_name}, {competition_title}, {site_name}

Results Published

Sent to members when competition results are available.

Available merge tags: {member_name}, {competition_title}, {results_page}, {site_name}

Detailed Results

Sent to each member with their personal results. The {results_table} tag is replaced with the ranked scores for their images.

Available merge tags: {member_name}, {competition_title}, {results_table}, {site_name}

Submission Confirmed

Sent when a member successfully uploads an image.

Available merge tags: {member_name}, {member_grade}, {competition_title}, {category_name}, {current_count}, {quota}, {site_name}
