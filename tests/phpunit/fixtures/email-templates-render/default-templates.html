Email Templates

Customize the email templates sent to members. Use merge tags to personalize messages.

Upload link

Sent to members with a link to upload their images.

Available merge tags:

  • {member_name} The member's name.
  • {competition_title} The competition's title.
  • {site_name} This site's name.
  • {upload_link} The member's own upload link.
  • {voting_page} The voting page.
  • {fee_info} The competition's entry fee and how to pay it. Empty when there's no fee.
  • {voting_info} A paragraph saying where members can vote. Empty when judges score the competition.
  • {archive_info} A paragraph saying whether the member's images show in the past competitions, with a link to their entries page to change it. Empty while past competitions are off.

Fills in the default subject and body. Save the email templates to keep them.
